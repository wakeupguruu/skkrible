Draw & guess

  1. Create a room and share its invite link.
  2. The host sets rounds, drawing time, word choices, difficulty, and letter hints on the board.
  3. Choose a secret word when it’s your turn. Draw it without spelling it out.
  4. Type guesses below the board. Faster correct guesses earn more points.

Only successful guesses appear in the shared feed. A one-letter typo gets a private “close” hint. Turn points appear between drawings.
